Supernova

A personal planning assistant.

Supernova is a private tool its author built for their own use: a to-do list, a weekly planner and an assistant that reminds them of the replies they owe and the admin they have to do. It runs on the author's own computer and is used by the author alone. It is not offered to the public, and there is no account to create.

It connects to the author's own Google account to read their mail, calendar and spreadsheets, and to save email drafts they send themselves. How that data is handled is in the privacy policy.
